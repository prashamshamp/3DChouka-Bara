📜 Sacred Rules of Chowka Bara

  • 🐚 4 Cowrie Shells (Kavade): Mouth-up shells count as 1 each (1, 2, 3). All 4 mouth-up = 4 (Chowka) + Bonus Turn! All 4 back-up = 8 (Bara / Ashta) + Bonus Turn!
  • ⚔️ Capture (Cut) Rule: Landing on a single opponent on any non-crossed square sends that opponent straight back to their Home House and grants you a Bonus Toss!
  • 🛡️ Safe Katte Squares: The 4 starting houses and 4 inner crossed squares are sanctuaries—nobody can be captured there.
  • 🔓 Inner Ring Lock: You must capture at least 1 opponent ("Cut") before your pieces can enter the inner ring toward the Center Gaddi.
  • 👑 Victory: Be the first player to guide all 4 of your pieces into the Center Gaddi sanctuary by exact roll!
